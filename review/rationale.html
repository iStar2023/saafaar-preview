Owner review only · Public-page audit · November 2026

Premium stays in Kurdistan.

The redesign makes holiday stays Saafaar’s primary product, with property sales and longer-term rentals clearly separated into Saafaar Estates. This is a review prototype, not a replacement booking system or a proposed change to the mobile API.

1. Lead with a reason to travel

Direction A, landscape-led, is recommended: a full-width regional landscape creates a distinct destination identity, with one destination/date/guest search. Direction B uses architecture and an editorial split composition to emphasize the homes. A is the stronger brand introduction; B remains available on the directions page for comparison. Prototype imagery is illustrative, not evidence of actual listing appearance.

2. Remove conflicting product signals

The audited homepage mixes stays with property sales, and its city links open estate archives. The redesign gives stays and Estates separate navigation and routes. Each city has distinct copy: Erbil’s citadel and contemporary neighborhoods, Sulaymaniyah’s cultural life and mountain setting, and Duhok’s hillside homes and slower pace. Collection and destination tiles omit prices consistently.

3. Correct prices without changing their meaning

The public search lists four sample stays with numeric rates 30, 45, 65, and 120. Per the owner’s confirmed explanation, these are USD amounts with an incorrect IQD label. USD is primary, “/ night” is consistent, and the hero’s starting rate derives from the minimum listing: $30 / night. IQD conversion is approximate at an illustrative 1 USD = 1,310 IQD, rounded for display. Final exchange rate, weekend rates, fees, deposits, and rental periods need approval.

4. Replace unsupported trust claims with transparent review content

The four homepage testimonials are sample content, while the inspected stay has zero reviews. No quotes, scores, guest identities, or invented bookings are reproduced. A clearly marked “Reviews coming soon / for owner review” block replaces testimonials. Verification, support, and secure-booking language describes the proposed service promise and requires owner sign-off.

5. Keep the journey clear and local

Search, date/guest selection, a sticky price summary, a +964 contact step, and a WhatsApp preference make the request process understandable. Every submission is simulated locally: no payment, message, booking, or agent inquiry is sent. English, Sorani, and Arabic demonstrate language and RTL behavior; all translated text is for native review, not approved production copy.

Live admin, hosting, DNS, plugins, database and REST API remain untouched. Implementation handoff · Hero directions · Return to prototype
